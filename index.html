What this integration does

This Google OAuth client lets its owner sign in to Google and authorize the third-party GSync plugin to synchronize Obsidian notes with Google Drive. Files are transferred by GSync between the user's device and Google's API without an application-operated sync server.

  • Synchronizes notes and attachments in a selected Google Drive folder.
  • Supports uploads, downloads and deletion synchronization when configured by the user.
  • Uses Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication.
  • Does not offer a public sign-up service or invite other users.

Permission notice: GSync currently requests full Google Drive access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive), even though its syncing behavior is configured to use a particular folder. Only authorize this OAuth client if you trust the plugin and understand the requested permission.
